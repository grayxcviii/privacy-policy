Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ComeCrew ("ComeCrew", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the ComeCrew mobile app (the "App"). ComeCrew helps you find people who are free, join or create activities nearby, chat with your crew and share stories.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy and to our Terms of Use.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us in readable form). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address.
  • Profile information: profile photo, display name, bio, interests, date of birth (used to show your age; your date of birth itself is never shown to others), hometown and current city, and other details you choose to add.
  • Activities: activities you create or join, including title, description, photo, time, meeting place or online joining details, participants, your check-ins and your status in the activity.
  • Messages and media: chat messages, photos, videos, voice messages, stickers, reactions, and stories you send or post, including replies and reactions to other people's stories.
  • Social connections: the people you follow, friend requests you send or accept, and invitations you send.
  • Reports, blocks and support: content and users you report (including the reason and any note you add), users you block, and messages you send to us. When you report something, we keep a copy of the reported content so that we can review it, even if it is later edited or deleted.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: a random identifier created when you install the App, your platform (iOS or Android), time zone and language. We use it to keep you signed in on each device, let you see and sign out of your devices, and show times and ages correctly.
  • Push token: a token from Apple/Google (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver notifications.
  • Usage and log data: technical logs such as request times and errors, used to keep the service running and secure. Our logs do not contain your messages, email address, tokens or location.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Provide the core features: discovering nearby activities and people, joining activities, chat, stories, the live map and check-in.
  • Calculate your trust score (the share of finished activities you checked in to) and the number of activities you completed.
  • Send notifications you have allowed.
  • Keep the community safe: review reports, enforce our Terms of Use, block abusive accounts, and prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • Fix bugs, maintain and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. What other users can see

  • Your profile: name, photo, age, bio, interests, hometown and current city (if you added them), trust score, number of completed activities, and your friends and followers.
  • Your distance from them, and how many activities you have done together.
  • Activities you create, and the fact that you joined an activity, are visible to other users who can view that activity. When you mark yourself as free, people nearby can see that you are free and what you would like to do.
  • Messages in a chat are visible to members of that chat.
  • Stories can be viewed by any signed-in ComeCrew user while they are up, and appear in your friends' story row. As the owner, you can see which of your friends viewed your story and the reactions they sent; other viewers are only counted. Stories you pin stay on your profile until you unpin or delete them.
  • Your live location is visible only to members of the same activity, from one hour before it starts until it ends.
  • People you block cannot see your profile, activities, stories or messages, and you cannot see theirs. They are not told that you blocked them.

4. End-to-end encrypted messages

You can turn on end-to-end encryption in Settings → Privacy. When enabled, messages in supported chats (personal chats and activity groups with up to 50 active members) are encrypted on your device, and we cannot read their content. Your private keys are stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore). If you set a backup PIN, an encrypted backup of your keys is stored on our servers and can only be unlocked with your PIN. Community group chats, larger groups and replies to stories are not end-to-end encrypted; the App tells you when a chat is not encrypted. If you report an encrypted message, the App sends us the text of that message so that we can review it.

6. Data storage and security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Chat and story media are private and served through short-lived signed links.
  • Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection.
  • No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

7. Data retention

  • We keep your account information while your account is active.
  • Stories are removed 24 hours after posting, unless you pin them to your profile; pinned stories stay until you unpin or delete them.
  • Chat messages and their media are deleted automatically 90 days after they are sent.
  • Notifications are deleted after 2 months.
  • Live map positions are deleted when the activity ends.
  • Reports: the copy of reported content is deleted 90 days after the report; a record that the report was made is kept for safety purposes.
  • When you delete your account, your profile is hidden from other users immediately and your personal information is permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days, except where we need to keep some data for a limited period to comply with law, resolve disputes, or handle safety reports.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the App: open Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm your identity, and confirm deletion.
  • Without the App: follow the steps at our account deletion page.

Deleting your account also removes ComeCrew data stored on your device, including your encryption keys, and revokes Sign in with Apple access if you used it. This action cannot be undone.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update your profile information in the App.
  • Turn location, camera, microphone, photo and notification permissions on or off in your device settings.
  • Turn live location sharing and end-to-end encryption on or off in Settings → Privacy, and choose which notifications you receive in Settings → Notifications.
  • Block or report other users, their stories and activities; manage blocked people in Settings → Blocked people.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

10. Children's privacy

ComeCrew is not intended for anyone under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete their account and data.
