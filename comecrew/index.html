ComeCrew Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ComeCrew ("ComeCrew", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the ComeCrew mobile app (the "App"). ComeCrew helps you find people who are free, join or create activities nearby, chat with your crew and share stories.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us in readable form). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address.
  • Profile information: profile photo, display name, bio, interests and other details you choose to add.
  • Activities: activities you create or join, including title, description, photo, time, meeting place and participants.
  • Messages and media: chat messages, photos, videos, voice messages and stories you send or post.
  • Reports and support: content you report, users you block, and messages you send to us.

1.2 Information collected with your permission

PermissionWhat we use it for
Location Show activities and free people near you, check you in at the meeting point, and share your position on the live map with members of an activity you joined, only while that activity is taking place. You can turn live location sharing off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
CameraTake photos and videos for stories, chats and your profile.
MicrophoneRecord voice messages and the sound of videos.
Photo libraryChoose photos and videos to share, and save photos from chats to your device.
NotificationsSend you alerts about messages, activities, friends and stories.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.

Before photos are uploaded, the App compresses them and removes embedded metadata such as GPS location (EXIF).

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: a random identifier created when you install the App, device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Push token: a token from Apple/Google (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver notifications.
  • Usage and log data: technical logs such as request times and errors, used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Provide the core features: discovering nearby activities and people, joining activities, chat, stories, live map and check-in.
  • Send notifications you have allowed.
  • Keep the community safe: review reports, enforce our rules, and prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • Fix bugs, maintain and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. What other users can see

  • Your profile (name, photo, bio, interests) is visible to other ComeCrew users.
  • Activities you create, and the fact that you joined an activity, are visible to other users who can view that activity.
  • Messages in a group chat are visible to members of that chat. Stories are visible to the audience you choose.
  • Your live location is visible only to members of the same activity, and only during that activity.

4. End-to-end encrypted messages

You can turn on end-to-end encryption in Settings → Privacy. When enabled, messages in supported chats (personal chats and activity groups with up to 50 active members) are encrypted on your device, and we cannot read their content. Your private keys are stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore). If you set a backup PIN, an encrypted backup of your keys is stored on our servers and can only be unlocked with your PIN. Community group chats and larger groups are not end-to-end encrypted; the App tells you when a chat is not encrypted.

5. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run ComeCrew, under contracts that limit their use of your data:

ProviderPurpose
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Messaging)Sign-in and push notifications
Google Sign-In, Sign in with AppleSigning in with your Google or Apple account
Cloudflare (Workers, R2 storage)Hosting our backend and storing photos, videos and voice messages
Map tile and geocoding providersDisplaying maps and turning coordinates into place names

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

6. Data storage and security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The App uses certificate pinning for our API.
  • Private chat and story media are served through short-lived signed links.
  • Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection.
  • No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

7. Data retention

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • Stories are available for a limited time and then removed from view.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, except where we need to keep some data for a limited period to comply with law, resolve disputes, or handle safety reports.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the App: open Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm your identity, and confirm deletion.
  • Without the App: follow the steps at our account deletion page.

Deleting your account also removes ComeCrew data stored on your device, including your encryption keys, and revokes Sign in with Apple access if you used it. This action cannot be undone.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update your profile information in the App.
  • Turn location, camera, microphone, photo and notification permissions on or off in your device settings.
  • Turn live location sharing and end-to-end encryption on or off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Block or report other users.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

10. Children's privacy

ComeCrew is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you in the App or by other means and update the effective date above.
