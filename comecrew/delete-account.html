Delete Your ComeCrew Account

Last updated: November 20, 2026

You can delete your ComeCrew account and its associated data at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the ComeCrew app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and tap Settings.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your identity: enter your password, or sign in again with Google or Apple.
  5. Tap Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out on that device.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, send us a deletion request:

To: longnv.main@gmail.com
Subject: ComeCrew account deletion request
Include: the email address you use to sign in to ComeCrew, and the sign-in method (email & password, Google, or Apple).

Please send the request from the same email address linked to your account so we can verify that you own it. If you signed in with Apple's "Hide My Email", tell us your relay address (…@privaterelay.appleid.com). We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity. We will process your request within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in information (email, name, Google/Apple sign-in link)
  • Your profile: name, photo, bio and interests
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Activities you created, and your participation in activities you joined
  • Your chat messages, photos, videos and voice messages
  • Your stories
  • Your location data and live location history
  • Your notification and push token data
  • Your end-to-end encryption keys and their encrypted backup

When you delete in the app, ComeCrew data stored on your device (including encryption keys in your device's secure storage) is also removed, and Sign in with Apple access is revoked if you used it.

What may be kept, and for how long

DataWhyHow long
Reports you made or that were made about you, and related safety records Protecting the community and preventing abuse Up to 90 days
Server logs and backups Security and recovering from failures; removed automatically as backups rotate Up to 30 days

Messages you sent in group chats may be removed or shown as coming from a deleted user. Copies that other users saved themselves (for example, screenshots or photos saved to their device) are outside our control.
